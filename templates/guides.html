Rental & Buying Guides

Practical tips to help you rent or buy safely and confidently in Mombasa. Use these guides together with verified listings and manager profiles on HouseFinder Mombasa.

  • Always view the property in person before sending any money.
  • Confirm the identity of the manager or agent and ask for written agreements.
  • Use official channels for payments and keep all receipts and messages safely.
  • Check water, power, security, and accessibility before you commit.

The right choice depends on your long-term plans, savings, and how long you want to stay in the area.

  • Renting offers flexibility and lower upfront costs.
  • Buying can build long-term value but needs more planning and due diligence.
  • Consider total monthly cost, not just rent or mortgage (utilities, service charge, etc.).

  • Be careful of offers that look too cheap for prime locations.
  • Do not send deposits to personal numbers without proper documentation.
  • Insist on viewing and signing written agreements before paying large amounts.
  • Use trusted platforms and verified managers where possible.

  • Have your identification, contacts, and references ready when applying.
  • Keep copies of signed tenancy agreements and payment records.
  • Clarify who is responsible for repairs, utilities, and service charges in writing.
