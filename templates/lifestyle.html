Lifestyle & Home Ideas

Explore ideas to make your new house feel like home, from coastal-inspired decor to smart space usage in apartments and bedsitters.

Coastal-Themed Decor

Use light colours, natural materials, and simple decor to reflect Mombasa’s coastal feel without spending too much.

  • Light curtains to maximise natural light.
  • Simple art and wall hangings instead of heavy fixtures.
  • Neutral furniture with a few coloured cushions or throws.
Making Small Spaces Work

Many city apartments and bedsitters are compact. Smart choices can make them feel larger.

  • Use multi-purpose furniture like sofa beds or storage ottomans.
  • Keep walkways clear and avoid overfilling the room.
  • Use vertical shelves for books, decor, or kitchen storage.
Comfort, Safety, and Community

Lifestyle is more than just furniture. The right neighbourhood and building affect your daily life.

  • Check noise levels at different times of day.
  • Consider access to public transport, shops, schools, or workplaces.
  • Ask about security, lighting, and how visitors are handled.
  • Think about ventilation and cooling, especially in hotter months.
Featured Neighbourhoods
  • Nyali: Popular for apartments near the beach and malls.
  • Bamburi: Mix of budget and mid-range housing with good access roads.
  • Mombasa CBD: Suitable if you want to be close to offices and services.
